Privacy policy

Updated: October 6, 2026

No data collectedNo account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers. inksumi connects to no server.

In short

inksumi collects no personal data. The app has no account, contains no ads, analytics or trackers, and connects to no server. What you paint stays on your device.

What is stored on the device

  • Your sheets: the painting (layers, water still wet), the paper, the pans, the title and a thumbnail.
  • Your settings (brushes, app preferences).
  • For stylus tuning, the last twenty strokes (positions, pressure, tilt) in a local file.

These files stay in the app's private space. They are included in the device backup (iCloud or computer) according to your iOS settings, managed by Apple. The publisher never has access to them.

Permissions

  • Photos (add only): to save an exported image to your photo library, when you ask. inksumi does not read your photo library.
  • Device motion: the iPad's tilt makes the water run and orients the light on the paper. It is used in the moment and never stored.
  • Stylus: pressure, tilt and orientation shape the stroke; they are used only for painting.

You can remove access to Photos at any time in Settings.

Sharing

An image or file leaves the app only when you share it yourself (iOS share sheet), to the destination you choose. inksumi sends nothing on its own.

Children

inksumi collects no data, including children's data.

Your rights

Since no data is collected, the publisher holds nothing about you. You stay in control of your sheets: delete one with a long press in the gallery, or all of them by deleting the app.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to the publisher from the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page, with its date.

Your sheets

Backup

Sheets are included in the device backup (iCloud or computer), according to your iOS settings.

Erasing

Deleting a sheet (long press in the gallery) erases it from the device; deleting the app erases all sheets.

Your work belongs to you

What you paint with inksumi is yours; the app claims no rights to your images.
