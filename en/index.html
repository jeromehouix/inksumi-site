Inksumi

Simulated watercolor and ink, on iPad

Inksumi is a painting studio for iPad where the medium is simulated: water flows, pigment settles, paper drinks. You paint in watercolor and ink, and also in gouache, acrylic and dry media, on papers that each have their own character.

A medium that behaves like the real thing

Watercolor

Wet-in-wet, soft blends, blooms and cauliflowers, dark edges, granulation: nothing is drawn in advance, everything comes from water flowing and drying on the grain of the paper. The brush is a bundle of hairs that bends, spreads and runs dry.

At hand: water and pigment load, drying, hair dryer, the tilt of the sheet, gum arabic and ox gall.

Ink and sumi

Sumi in tones, a tip that splits, a dry brush that breaks on the grain. Colored inks, defined by their spectrum, separate in the wet.

Gouache, acrylic, dry media

Paint follows the tooth of the paper, acrylic dries into a sealing film; pencil, pastel, chalk and charcoal bleed into a wash that is still damp. Colors mix like pigments, over seven bands of the spectrum.

Three Japanese workshops

Papers and sketchbooks

Rough, fine grain, Xuan, Yupo, canvas: each paper drinks, dries and cockles in its own way, deckle edge included. Sheets are kept in sketchbooks and leporellos that become books in PDF or EPUB; for printing, a 16-bit TIFF or a layered PSD.

Privacy policy

In short

inksumi collects no personal data. The app has no account, contains no ads, analytics or trackers, and connects to no server. What you paint stays on your device.

What is stored on the device

  • Your sheets and sketchbooks: the painting (layers, water still wet), the paper, the pans, the title, a thumbnail, the time-lapse and the images you placed on them (reference photo, stencil, relief).
  • Your palettes, your carved blocks and your settings (brushes, signature, app preferences).
  • For stylus and breath tuning, the last twenty strokes (positions, pressure, tilt) and the last levels heard by the microphone (numbers, no sound) in local files.

These files stay in the app's private space. They are included in the device backup (iCloud or computer) according to your iOS settings, managed by Apple. The publisher never has access to them.

Permissions

  • Photos: to save an exported image to your photo library, when you ask, and to open a photo you choose, as a reference next to the sheet or as a stencil. inksumi reads only the photos you choose; they stay on the device.
  • Microphone: in suminagashi, when you turn on “Blow into the mic”, the sound level pushes the ink across the water. No sound is recorded or sent; the microphone turns off with the option or when you leave the bath.
  • Files: no permission; inksumi opens only the files you choose (an SVG shape, an image).
  • Device motion: the iPad's tilt makes the water run and orients the light on the paper. It is used in the moment and never stored.
  • Stylus: pressure, tilt and orientation shape the stroke; they are used only for painting.

You can remove access to Photos or the microphone at any time in Settings.

Sharing

An image or file leaves the app only when you share it yourself (iOS share sheet), to the destination you choose. inksumi sends nothing on its own. If you have set a signature (Share › Signature), your name and your rights notice are written into exported files.

Children

inksumi collects no data, including children's data.

Your rights

Since no data is collected, the publisher holds nothing about you. You stay in control of your sheets: delete one with a long press in the gallery (it stays 30 days in Recently Deleted, then is erased), or all of them by deleting the app.
